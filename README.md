# Templeton Global Search Mining Leadership Turnover Index

Quarterly counts of chief executive (CEO), chief financial officer (CFO), chief operating officer (COO), technical chief and exploration chief changes at 203 listed mining companies with a market capitalisation of US$500 million or more, 1 January 2025 to 27 October 2026. This is the first edition. The next edition follows in March 2027.

The Templeton Global Search Mining Leadership Turnover Index is compiled by Templeton Global Search, a retained executive search firm working solely in mining, critical minerals and the energy transition. Full findings: https://templetonglobalsearch.com/insights/mining-executive-committee-turnover/

## Files

- `published-totals.csv`: totals by quarter and role, as published on the findings page.
- `METHOD.md` and `method-note.pdf`: which companies, roles, changes and evidence are counted, and how rates are calculated.

## Columns in published-totals.csv

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| quarter | Calendar quarter. Q4 2026 covers 1 to 27 October 2026 only and is not comparable with full quarters. |
| role | CEO, CFO, COO, CTO (technical chief) or CXO (exploration chief). |
| departures | Departures announced in the quarter. |
| appointments | Permanent appointments announced in the quarter. |
| interim_appointments | Interim appointments announced in the quarter. |
| turnover_rate_pct | Departures divided by rate_base_companies, as a percentage. |
| rate_base_companies | Companies tracked for that role: all 203 for CEO and CFO; only companies that have the role for COO, CTO and CXO. |

Totals only: the index does not name companies.
